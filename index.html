How it works

Give each person a limit, then add gifts under their name. Every gift comes off that person's limit and off your total at the same time, so you always know what is left for them and what is left overall.

Your list is saved on this device, in this browser. Nothing is uploaded and there is no account. If you clear your browser data the list goes with it, so use Back up my data before you switch phones.
